ℹ️ About AI Interview Coach

AI Interview Coach is a smart, AI-powered interview preparation tool built using Flask and modern web technologies. It helps users analyze resumes, automatically generate role-specific questions, and receive instant feedback — simulating a real interview environment.

Designed with simplicity, intelligence, and a futuristic aesthetic, this platform empowers you to build confidence, improve your communication, and succeed in your next interview.
